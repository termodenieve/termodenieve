### 🐎 SIGCAZ — Cabalgata por la Toma de Zacatecas

[Repository](https://github.com/Armandiux2324/SIGCAZ)

A management system developed for the Cabalgata por la Toma de Zacatecas, a government tourism event. The project focuses on participant registration, event administration, and digital identification through QR codes.

It integrates a Laravel backend API with database management and automated processes, alongside Angular and Ionic frontends, to support the organization and control of event participants.

*Tech Stack:* Laravel, PHP, MySQL, REST API, Angular, Ionic, QR Codes.
